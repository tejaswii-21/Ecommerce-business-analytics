# Ecommerce-business-analytics

## Business Problem

An e-commerce business generates a large amount of transactional data from
its customers and products. However, raw transaction data alone does not
clearly explain how the business is performing, which customers and products
contribute the most revenue, which markets are performing well, or where
sales are declining.

The objective of this project is to analyse the company's e-commerce
transaction data to understand sales performance, customer behaviour,
product performance, market performance, and revenue trends.

Using SQL and Python, the project transforms raw transaction data into
meaningful business insights. The analysis investigates questions such as:

- Which products and customers generate the most revenue?
- Which countries or markets contribute most to the business?
- How are sales and revenue changing over time?
- Where is revenue declining, and to what extent?
- What factors may be contributing to changes in revenue?
- Which customers are most valuable to the business?
- Are there risks associated with customer or market concentration?

The final goal is to identify business opportunities, potential risks, and
actionable recommendations that can help management improve business
performance.
